    ADM DASHBOARD · WEB BETA 0.3

    New Eden, in your browser.

    An unofficial web companion built from SMT’s regional map snapshot. ADM and development indexes come directly from CCP’s public ESI service and refresh about every five minutes while this page is open.

    Your intel stays here. Imported files are read locally. Reports are held in this tab’s memory, not uploaded or shared. Settings and the last public ADM response are saved in this browser.

    Map routes use the bundled stargate snapshot—not wormholes or jump bridges. Verify routes in game. N/A means no applicable sovereignty ADM; — means missing data. Amber ADM values are cached or unverified. Intel rings expire after 15 minutes.

    Search supports full and partial system names. Intel matching requires complete names. EVE SSO, automatic folder watching, shared intel, and desktop overlays are not included.
